Field of an infinite line of charge

무한 선전하의 전기장

An infinite straight line carries uniform linear charge density λ\lambdaλ (C/m). Find E(r)E(r)E(r) at perpendicular distance rrr from the line.
한글
선전하 밀도 λ\lambdaλ. 거리 rrr 에서 EEE.
Back to list
Hints
  1. Cylindrical Gaussian surface coaxial with the line.
  2. No flux through end caps.
한글 힌트
  1. 동축 원통.
  2. 옆면만 플럭스.
Worked solution
Answer
E=λ/(2πε0r)E = \lambda/(2\pi\varepsilon_0 r)E=λ/(2πε0​r)
한글
E=λ/(2πε0r)E=\lambda/(2\pi\varepsilon_0 r)E=λ/(2πε0​r)
  1. Symmetry. E⃗\vec EE is radial (perpendicular to the line) and depends only on rrr.
  2. Gaussian cylinder. Radius rrr, length LLL: flux through curved wall E(r) (2πrL)E(r)\,(2\pi r L)E(r)(2πrL); end caps contribute 0.
  3. Enclosed charge. Qenc=λLQ_\mathrm{enc} = \lambda LQenc​=λL.
  4. Gauss’s law. E (2πrL)=λL/ε0⇒E=λ/(2πε0r)E\,(2\pi r L) = \lambda L/\varepsilon_0 \Rightarrow E = \lambda/(2\pi\varepsilon_0 r)E(2πrL)=λL/ε0​⇒E=λ/(2πε0​r).
  5. Direction. Outward from the line if λ>0\lambda>0λ>0.
