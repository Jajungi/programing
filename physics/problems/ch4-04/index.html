Crossed rods: potential on the symmetry axis

십자 막대 축 위 전위

Two thin rods of length ℓ\ellℓ each lie along the xxx and yyy axes, centered at the origin, with uniform charge 3 on each rod (total charge 2Q2Q2Q). Find the electrostatic potential at point PPP on the +z+z+z axis a distance zzz from the origin, with V(∞)=0V(\infty)=0V(∞)=0.
