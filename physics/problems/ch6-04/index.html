Capacitance of an isolated sphere

고립 구의 capacitance

Starting from V=kQ/RV = kQ/RV=kQ/R for an isolated conducting sphere of radius RRR, define capacitance and show C=4πε0RC = 4\pi\varepsilon_0 RC=4πε0​R. How does CCC scale if the radius doubles?
한글
V=kQ/RV=kQ/RV=kQ/R 에서 C=4πε0RC=4\pi\varepsilon_0 RC=4πε0​R 유도. RRR 2배 시 CCC?
Back to list
Hints
  1. C=Q/VC = Q/VC=Q/V by definition.
  2. Compare to parallel-plate C=ε0A/dC=\varepsilon_0 A/dC=ε0​A/d.
한글 힌트
  1. C=Q/VC=Q/VC=Q/V.
  2. V∝Q/RV\propto Q/RV∝Q/R.
Worked solution
Answer
C=4πε0RC = 4\pi\varepsilon_0 RC=4πε0​R; doubles when R→2RR\to 2RR→2R
한글
C=4πε0RC=4\pi\varepsilon_0 RC=4πε0​R; RRR 2배면 CCC 2배
  1. Definition. Capacitance is the proportionality Q=CVQ = C VQ=CV between stored charge magnitude and potential relative to infinity.
  2. Sphere potential. V=14πε0QRV = \dfrac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\dfrac{Q}{R}V=4πε0​1​RQ​.
  3. Solve for 0. C=Q/V=4πε0RC = Q/V = 4\pi\varepsilon_0 RC=Q/V=4πε0​R — depends only on geometry (and medium; here vacuum).
  4. Scaling. Doubling RRR doubles CCC; larger spheres hold more charge at the same VVV.
  5. Contrast. Parallel-plate CCC grows with area and shrinks with separation; all formulas count how much charge can be separated at a given ΔV\Delta VΔV.
