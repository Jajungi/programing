Ch.1 · Concept · #6 · Ch.1 Coulomb’s Law

Why electrostatic force dominates at atomic scales

원자 스케일에서 전기력이 우세한 이유

Compare the magnitudes of the electric and gravitational forces between an electron and a proton separated by r=1.0 A˚r = 1.0\,\mathrm{\AA}r=1.0A˚. Which dominates? Why do we still feel gravity in daily life?
한글
전자–양성자 거리 r=1.0 A˚r=1.0\,\mathrm{\AA}r=1.0A˚ 에서 전기력과 중력 크기 비교. 어느 쪽이 큰가? 일상에서 중력이 느껴지는 이유는?

Hints

  2. Macroscopic objects are nearly neutral; gravity adds without cancellation.
한글 힌트

  2. 거대 물체는 거의 전기 중성.
Worked solution
Answer
Fe/Fg∼1039F_e/F_g \sim 10^{39}Fe​/Fg​∼1039; gravity felt because matter is neutral macroscopically
한글
Fe/Fg∼1039F_e/F_g \sim 10^{39}Fe​/Fg​∼1039; 거시적 전기 중성
  1. Same 0 dependence. Both scale as 1/r21/r^21/r2, so the ratio Fe/FgF_e/F_gFe​/Fg​ is independent of rrr for this pair.
  2. Compute ratio. Fe/Fg=ke2/(Gmemp)≈2×1039F_e/F_g = k e^2/(G m_e m_p) \approx 2\times 10^{39}Fe​/Fg​=ke2/(Gme​mp​)≈2×1039 — electricity wins overwhelmingly at atomic separation.
  3. Binding atoms. The attractive Coulomb force sets atomic size; gravity between electron and proton is utterly negligible inside atoms.
  4. Everyday gravity. Bulk matter contains nearly equal positive and negative charge, so net electric forces cancel to high precision, while mass (hence gravity) adds up.
  5. Takeaway. Coulomb forces rule microphysics; gravity rules astronomy because astronomical bodies are electrically neutral overall.

  2. 원자. 쿨롱력이 원자 크기를 정함; 중력은 무시.
  3. 일상. 거시적으로 +/- 전하 상쇄, 질량(중력)은 누적.
  4. 정리. 미시=전기, 거시 천체=중성이라 중력.
