Force on a test charge between $\pm Q$

$\pm Q$ 사이 시험 전하의 힘

Point charges +Q+Q+Q and −Q-Q−Q are separated by distance ddd on the xxx axis (at x=±d/2x=\pm d/2x=±d/2). A test charge qqq sits on the perpendicular bisector at distance xxx from the midpoint (so its coordinates are (0,x)(0,x)(0,x)). Find the magnitude of the net force on qqq and state when it is directed toward the negative charge.
한글
+Q+Q+Q 와 −Q-Q−Q 가 xxx 축 위 ddd 만큼 떨어짐 (x=±d/2x=\pm d/2x=±d/2). 시험 전하 qqq 는 중점에서 수직 이등분선 위 거리 xxx ((0,x)(0,x)(0,x)). 합 힘의 크기와 −Q-Q−Q 쪽 방향 조건.

  1. Distances to each source: r=x2+(d/2)2r = \sqrt{x^2 + (d/2)^2}r=x2+(d/2)2​.
  2. Vertical components add; horizontal components cancel by symmetry.

  1. Geometry. Each source is distance r=x2+(d/2)2r = \sqrt{x^2+(d/2)^2}r=x2+(d/2)2​ from qqq. The line from +Q+Q+Q to qqq makes angle α\alphaα with the vertical, with sin⁡α=(d/2)/r\sin\alpha = (d/2)/rsinα=(d/2)/r.
  2. Forces. Magnitude from each charge: F0=k∣Qq∣/r2F_0 = k|Q q|/r^2F0​=k∣Qq∣/r2. Horizontal components cancel; vertical components along the bisector add.
  3. Net along bisector. Fnet=2F0sin⁡α=2 k∣Qq∣/r2⋅(d/2)/r=k∣Qq∣d/[x2+(d/2)2]3/2F_\mathrm{net} = 2 F_0 \sin\alpha = 2\,k|Q q|/r^2 \cdot (d/2)/r = k|Q q| d / [x^2+(d/2)^2]^{3/2}Fnet​=2F0​sinα=2k∣Qq∣/r2⋅(d/2)/r=k∣Qq∣d/[x2+(d/2)2]3/2.
  4. Direction. +Q+Q+Q repels qqq upward (if q>0q>0q>0) and −Q-Q−Q attracts upward as well when q>0q>0q>0 — net toward the negative charge side along −y-y−y if +Q+Q+Q is on the −x-x−x side; always toward the attractive partner for q>0q>0q>0.
