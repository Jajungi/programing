Electric field on the axis of a charged ring

대전 고리 축 위 전기장

A thin ring of radius RRR carries total charge QQQ uniformly spread on its circumference. Find the electric field magnitude EEE at a point on the axis a distance zzz from the center of the ring.
한글
반지름 RRR, 전하 QQQ 인 고리. 중심에서 축 위 zzz 지점의 EEE 크기.
Back to list
Hints
  1. Every charge element is distance R2+z2\sqrt{R^2+z^2}R2+z2​ from the axis point.
  2. Only the on-axis component survives by symmetry.
한글 힌트
  1. r=R2+z2r=\sqrt{R^2+z^2}r=R2+z2​.
  2. 축 성분만 남음.
Worked solution
Answer
E=14πε0Qz(R2+z2)3/2E = \dfrac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\dfrac{Q z}{(R^2+z^2)^{3/2}}E=4πε0​1​(R2+z2)3/2Qz​
한글
E=kQz/(R2+z2)3/2E = kQz/(R^2+z^2)^{3/2}E=kQz/(R2+z2)3/2
  1. Symmetry. Off-axis components cancel around the ring; net field points along z^\hat zz^.
  2. Element field. A charge dqdqdq produces dE⃗=k dq r^/r2d\vec E = k\,dq\,\hat r/r^2dE=kdqr^/r2 with r=R2+z2r=\sqrt{R^2+z^2}r=R2+z2​.
  3. Project on axis. dEz=dEcos⁡θ=dE⋅z/rdE_z = dE \cos\theta = dE \cdot z/rdEz​=dEcosθ=dE⋅z/r, so dEz=k dq z/(R2+z2)3/2dE_z = k\,dq\,z/(R^2+z^2)^{3/2}dEz​=kdqz/(R2+z2)3/2.
  4. Integrate charge. ∫dq=Q\int dq = Q∫dq=Q gives Ez=kQz/(R2+z2)3/2E_z = k Q z/(R^2+z^2)^{3/2}Ez​=kQz/(R2+z2)3/2.
  5. Checks. E(0)=0E(0)=0E(0)=0 (symmetry). For z≫Rz\gg Rz≫R, E≈kQ/z2E \approx kQ/z^2E≈kQ/z2 like a point charge.
