Electrostatic energy of a charged conducting sphere

대전 금속 구의 정전 에너지

A isolated conducting sphere of radius RRR carries total charge QQQ. Show that the stored energy is U=Q2/(8πε0R)U = Q^2/(8\pi\varepsilon_0 R)U=Q2/(8πε0​R).
한글
반지름 RRR, 전하 QQQ 고립 구. U=Q2/(8πε0R)U=Q^2/(8\pi\varepsilon_0 R)U=Q2/(8πε0​R).

Hints
  1. C=4πε0RC = 4\pi\varepsilon_0 RC=4πε0​R for an isolated sphere.
  2. Or integrate energy density u=ε0E2/2u=\varepsilon_0 E^2/2u=ε0​E2/2.

  1. C=4πε0RC=4\pi\varepsilon_0 RC=4πε0​R.

Worked solution
Answer
U=Q2/(8πε0R)U = Q^2/(8\pi\varepsilon_0 R)U=Q2/(8πε0​R)
한글
U=Q2/(8πε0R)U=Q^2/(8\pi\varepsilon_0 R)U=Q2/(8πε0​R)
  1. Capacitance. Isolated sphere: V=kQ/RV = kQ/RV=kQ/R, so C=Q/V=4πε0RC = Q/V = 4\pi\varepsilon_0 RC=Q/V=4πε0​R.
  2. Energy in capacitor form. U=12QV=12Q(Q/(4πε0R))U = \dfrac{1}{2} Q V = \dfrac{1}{2} Q (Q/(4\pi\varepsilon_0 R))U=21​QV=21​Q(Q/(4πε0​R)).
  3. Simplify. U=Q2/(8πε0R)U = Q^2/(8\pi\varepsilon_0 R)U=Q2/(8πε0​R).
  4. Alternate route. u=ε0E2/2u = \varepsilon_0 E^2/2u=ε0​E2/2, E=kQ/r2E=kQ/r^2E=kQ/r2 outside; integrate from RRR to ∞\infty∞ gives the same UUU.
  5. Scale. Energy grows like Q2Q^2Q2 and decreases with radius — smaller spheres store more energy at fixed QQQ.
