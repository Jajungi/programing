Dipole energy in a non-uniform field (concept + estimate)

쌍극자 전기 에너지

A dipole p⃗=3.0×10−29 C⋅m\vec p = 3.0\times 10^{-29}\,\mathrm{C\cdot m}p​=3.0×10−29C⋅m aligns with a uniform field E=2.0×105 N/CE = 2.0\times 10^5\,\mathrm{N/C}E=2.0×105N/C. Compute the dipole potential energy. If the field increases by 10%10\%10% along the dipole axis, estimate the change in UUU.

Hints
  1. U=−pEU = -pEU=−pE when aligned.
  2. Use ΔU≈p ΔE\Delta U \approx p\,\Delta EΔU≈pΔE for small changes.
한글 힌트
  1. U=−pEU=-pEU=−pE.
  2. ΔU≈−pΔE\Delta U\approx -p\Delta EΔU≈−pΔE.
Worked solution
Answer
U=−6.0×10−24 JU = -6.0\times 10^{-24}\,\mathrm{J}U=−6.0×10−24J; ΔU≈−6.0×10−25 J\Delta U \approx -6.0\times 10^{-25}\,\mathrm{J}ΔU≈−6.0×10−25J
한글
U=−6.0×10−24 JU=-6.0\times 10^{-24}\,\mathrm{J}U=−6.0×10−24J
  1. Aligned energy. U=−p⃗⋅E⃗=−pEU = -\vec p\cdot\vec E = -pEU=−p​⋅E=−pE (negative means stable alignment).
  2. Numerical. U=−(3.0×10−29)(2.0×105)=−6.0×10−24 JU = -(3.0\times 10^{-29})(2.0\times 10^5) = -6.0\times 10^{-24}\,\mathrm{J}U=−(3.0×10−29)(2.0×105)=−6.0×10−24J.
  3. Field increase. ΔE=0.10E=2.0×104 N/C\Delta E = 0.10 E = 2.0\times 10^4\,\mathrm{N/C}ΔE=0.10E=2.0×104N/C.
  4. Energy shift. ΔU≈−p ΔE=−6.0×10−25 J\Delta U \approx -p\,\Delta E = -6.0\times 10^{-25}\,\mathrm{J}ΔU≈−pΔE=−6.0×10−25J (more negative = more bound).
  5. Context. In non-uniform fields, dipoles also feel a net force F⃗≈(p⃗⋅∇)E⃗\vec F \approx (\vec p\cdot\nabla)\vec EF≈(p​⋅∇)E; here we only estimate ΔU\Delta UΔU in a nearly uniform region.
