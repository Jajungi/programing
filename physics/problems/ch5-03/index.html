U≈−0.11 JU\approx -0.11\,\mathrm{J}U≈−0.11J
  1. Assembly picture. Fix q1q_1q1​, bring q2q_2q2​ from infinity slowly; external work equals increase in system energy.
  2. Pair energy. U=kq1q2/rU = k q_1 q_2/rU=kq1​q2​/r (sign included). Here q1q2<0q_1 q_2<0q1​q2​<0, so U<0U<0U<0 (bound pair).
  3. Numbers. U=(8.99×109)(2.0×10−6)(−3.0×10−6)/(0.50)≈−0.108 JU = (8.99\times 10^9)(2.0\times 10^{-6})(-3.0\times 10^{-6})/(0.50) \approx -0.108\,\mathrm{J}U=(8.99×109)(2.0×10−6)(−3.0×10−6)/(0.50)≈−0.108J.
  4. Work sign. External agent must do negative work Wext=U≈−0.11 JW_\mathrm{ext} = U \approx -0.11\,\mathrm{J}Wext​=U≈−0.11J — the field helps pull opposite charges together.
  5. Magnitude note. ∣U∣≈0.11 J|U| \approx 0.11\,\mathrm{J}∣U∣≈0.11J is the energy released if you let the charges fly together from infinity.
