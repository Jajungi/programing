Field inside and outside a uniformly charged solid sphere

균일 대전 구의 전기장

A non-conducting solid sphere of radius RRR has uniform volume charge density ρ\rhoρ. Find E(r)E(r)E(r) for r<Rr<Rr<R and r>Rr>Rr>R.
한글
반지름 RRR, 부피 밀도 ρ\rhoρ. r<Rr<Rr<R, r>Rr>Rr>R 에서 EEE.
Back to list
Hints
  1. Sphere Gaussian surface for r>Rr>Rr>R.
  2. For r<Rr<Rr<R, enclosed charge ρ (4/3)πr3\rho \,(4/3)\pi r^3ρ(4/3)πr3.
한글 힌트
  1. r>Rr>Rr>R: 점전하.
  2. r<Rr<Rr<R: Q∝r3Q\propto r^3Q∝r3.
Worked solution
Answer
E=ρr/(3ε0)E = \rho r/(3\varepsilon_0)E=ρr/(3ε0​) for r<Rr<Rr<R; E=ρR3/(3ε0r2)E = \rho R^3/(3\varepsilon_0 r^2)E=ρR3/(3ε0​r2) for r>Rr>Rr>R

  1. Outside (0). Treat as point charge Q=ρ(4/3)πR3Q = \rho (4/3)\pi R^3Q=ρ(4/3)πR3: E=Q/(4πε0r2)=ρR3/(3ε0r2)E = Q/(4\pi\varepsilon_0 r^2) = \rho R^3/(3\varepsilon_0 r^2)E=Q/(4πε0​r2)=ρR3/(3ε0​r2).

  5. Graph. Linear rise inside, 1/r21/r^21/r2 fall outside — typical Gauss’s-law exercise pattern.
