Net Coulomb force on a fourth charge

네 번째 전하에 작용하는 합 전기력

Four point charges are fixed in the xyxyxy-plane. Particle 4 (charge q4=+3.20×10−19 Cq_4 = +3.20\times 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}q4​=+3.20×10−19C) is at the origin.

• Particle 2 (q2=+3.20×10−19 Cq_2 = +3.20\times 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}q2​=+3.20×10−19C) lies on the +y+y+y axis at distance d2=2.00 cmd_2 = 2.00\,\mathrm{cm}d2​=2.00cm.
• Particle 3 (q3=+6.40×10−19 Cq_3 = +6.40\times 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}q3​=+6.40×10−19C) lies on the +x+x+x axis at distance d3=2.00 cmd_3 = 2.00\,\mathrm{cm}d3​=2.00cm.
• Particle 1 (q1=−3.20×10−19 Cq_1 = -3.20\times 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}q1​=−3.20×10−19C) is in the third quadrant at distance d1=3.00 cmd_1 = 3.00\,\mathrm{cm}d1​=3.00cm from the origin; the line from the origin to particle 1 makes angle θ1=35.0°\theta_1 = 35.0°θ1​=35.0° with the negative xxx-axis.

Find (a) the magnitude and (b) the direction of the net electrostatic force on particle 4 due to the other three particles.
한글
네 점전하가 xyxyxy 평면에 고정되어 있다. 입자 4 (q4=+3.20×10−19 Cq_4=+3.20\times 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}q4​=+3.20×10−19C)는 원점.

• 입자 2 (q2=+3.20×10−19 Cq_2=+3.20\times 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}q2​=+3.20×10−19C)는 +y+y+y 축, d2=2.00 cmd_2=2.00\,\mathrm{cm}d2​=2.00cm.
• 입자 3 (q3=+6.40×10−19 Cq_3=+6.40\times 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}q3​=+6.40×10−19C)는 +x+x+x 축, d3=2.00 cmd_3=2.00\,\mathrm{cm}d3​=2.00cm.
• 입자 1 (q1=−3.20×10−19 Cq_1=-3.20\times 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}q1​=−3.20×10−19C)는 제3사분면, d1=3.00 cmd_1=3.00\,\mathrm{cm}d1​=3.00cm, 원점에서 입자 1로의 선이 음의 xxx축과 θ1=35.0°\theta_1=35.0°θ1​=35.0°.
