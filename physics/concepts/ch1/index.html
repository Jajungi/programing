Overview · 개요

Electrostatics begins with Coulomb’s law: how stationary charges exert forces on one another. Ordinary matter is built from nuclei and electrons; the electric force binds electrons to nuclei and holds atoms together in molecules and solids. In this course we focus on electrostatic forces (charges at rest or moving slowly); magnetic and full electromagnetic effects come later.

정전기학은 쿨롱의 법칙으로 시작합니다. 물질은 원자·전자로 이루어지며, 전기력이 전자를 핵에 묶고 분자·고체를 형성합니다. 이 학기 구간은 정지/저속 전하(정전기)이고, 자기·전자기는 이후에 다룹니다. 시험: 벡터 합, SI 상수, 양자화·보존, 도체/절연체, 마찰·유도 충전 순서와 부호.

Why electrons do not collapse into the nucleus · 전자가 핵으로 떨어지지 않는 이유

Classically, attractive Coulomb force between proton and electron would pull them together. Quantum mechanics prevents collapse: confining an electron to a smaller region forces larger momentum uncertainty (Heisenberg):

Δx Δp≥ℏ2\Delta x\,\Delta p \ge \frac{\hbar}{2}ΔxΔp≥2ℏ​

So a stable atom balances electric attraction and quantum “pressure.” The same electric force also binds atoms in molecules and crystals — essentially all of macroscopic structure.

고전적으로는 흡인 Coulomb 힘만 있으면 전자가 핵에 붙어야 하지만, 불확정성 원리 ΔxΔp≥ℏ/2\Delta x\Delta p\ge\hbar/2ΔxΔp≥ℏ/2 때문에 더 작은 영역에 가두면 운동량·에너지가 커져 **안정 궤도(양자)**가 됩니다. 전기력은 분자·결정 결합에도 동일하게 작용합니다.

Charge quantization · 전하의 양자화

Observed charge on isolated particles is quantized: q=neq = n eq=ne with integer nnn (in this course). Quarks have fractional charges (±e/3\pm e/3±e/3, ±2e/3\pm 2e/3±2e/3) but are confined inside hadrons — you do not see free fractional charge in lab.

Millikan oil-drop experiment · Millikan 기름방울 실험

Robert Millikan (1910s) measured charges on tiny oil droplets in an electric field (balancing gravity and drag). Key result:

  • Every measured droplet charge was ±e\pm e±e, ±2e\pm 2e±2e, ±3e\pm 3e±3e, …
  • Never values like ±1.45e\pm 1.45e±1.45e or ±2.28e\pm 2.28e±2.28e

This strongly supported elementary charge eee as the natural unit. Symbol qqq is standard for charge as a variable.

Setup idea: Spray oil, pick a droplet, adjust field until motion is uniform → infer qqq from qE=mgqE = mgqE=mg (plus viscosity corrections in the full analysis).

Millikan 실험: 기름방울 전하가 항상 eee의 정수배(±e,±2e,…\pm e,\pm 2e,\ldots±e,±2e,…), 임의 분수는 없음 → 기본 전하 eee 입증. 변수 기호 qqq. 시험: “양자화”의 의미 = 관측 전하는 nenene.

Charging by friction · 마찰에 의한 충전

Discovered historically through rubbing amber or glass with fur/cloth: sparks, attraction of straw/feathers.

Mechanism: Contact transfers electrons between materials (triboelectric effect). The object that gains electrons becomes negatively charged; the other becomes positive. You must know which material tends to lose electrons in a given pair (lecture tables for glass vs silk, etc.) — do not memorize every series entry, but follow the electron flow in word problems.

Exam tip: Friction charging requires contact; net charge on each object is equal and opposite if they were neutral initially (conservation).

마찰 충전: 전자 이동 — 전자를 얻은 쪽 (−), 잃은 쪽 (+). 유리·모피 등 어디로 전자가 갔는지 추적. 처음 중성이면 두 물체 순전하 크기 같고 부호 반대(보존).

Charging by induction · 유도에 의한 충전

Different from friction: a charged rod (e.g. + on glass) polarizes a nearby neutral conductor — negative charge attracted toward rod, positive pushed away — but net charge still zero.

Standard steps (get net charge on conductor):

  1. Bring charged rod near neutral conductor (induces separation).
  2. Ground the far side (electrons leave or enter from Earth).
  3. Remove ground while rod is still present (trapped charge cannot return).
  4. Remove rod — conductor keeps net charge opposite to rod’s sign.

Applications: electrostatic photocopiers, Van de Graaff demos. Induction can charge without touching the rod to the object with the same sign as the rod.

**유도 충전:** 대전 막대가 중성 도체를 **분극**(순전하 0). **접지 → 접지 제거(막대 유지) → 막대 제거** 순서로 **막대와 반대 부호**의 순전하 획득. **복사기** 등 응용. 막대와 직접 접촉하지 않아도 충전 가능 — 단계 순서가 시험 포인트.
