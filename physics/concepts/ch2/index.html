Overview · 개요

The electric field E⃗\vec EE mediates the force between charges. A charged body creates a field in the space around it; a second charge feels F⃗=qE⃗\vec F = q\vec EF=qE. When a source charge moves, the force on a distant charge cannot change instantly — a disturbance propagates at speed ccc. Fields carry energy and momentum (important for waves and circuits later).

전기장 E⃗\vec EE는 전하 사이 힘의 매개체입니다. F⃗=qE⃗\vec F=q\vec EF=qE. 전하 이동 시 힘 변화는 유한 속도로 전파됩니다. 장은 에너지·운동량을 가집니다. 시험: E⃗\vec EE 정의, 점전하, 전기력선, 링/판/평행판, 쌍극자 τ⃗=p⃗×E⃗\vec\tau=\vec p\times\vec Eτ=p​×E, U=−p⃗⋅E⃗U=-\vec p\cdot\vec EU=−p​⋅E.

From action-at-a-distance to fields · 작용-at-a-distance에서 장으로

Newton worried about gravity acting without contact. The modern view: each charge produces an electric field; other charges interact with the local field value. This avoids “instant action” puzzles when charges accelerate.

Scalar field: one number per point (T(x,y,z)T(x,y,z)T(x,y,z)). Vector field: one vector per point (E⃗\vec EE, g⃗\vec gg​).

현대 해석은 장이 중간 매개입니다. 스칼라 장 vs 벡터 장 구분 — 전기장은 벡터 장.

Electric field lines · 전기력선

  1. Tangent → direction of E⃗\vec EE; density of lines ∝∣E∣\propto |E|∝∣E∣.
  2. Lines begin on +++ (sources), end on −-− (sinks) or infinity.

Limitation (lecture warning): Lines are helpful for static pictures. For moving charges or changing fields, drawings can fail — use the vector field definition.

(each side; direction away from positive sheet).

Two infinite parallel plates +σ+\sigma+σ and −σ-\sigma−σ:

Fringing (finite plates) · 가장자리 fringe

Real plates are finite. Near the edges, field lines bulge outward — fringing — and EEE is not uniform. Lecture: in 2D sketches, homogeneity fails at plate ends. Problems say “neglect edge effects” when you may use E=σ/ε0E=\sigma/\varepsilon_0E=σ/ε0​ uniformly between plates.

**무한 한 장:** $E=\sigma/(2\varepsilon_0)$. **두 판 사이:** $E=\sigma/\varepsilon_0$. **유한 판**은 가장자리 **fringe**로 내부도 완전 균일하지 않음 — “가장자리 무시” 조건 확인.

Electric dipoles · 전기 쌍극자

Neutral object with separated +Q+Q+Q and −Q-Q−Q: net force zero in uniform E⃗\vec EE, but torque if p⃗\vec pp​ is not aligned.

p⃗=Qℓ⃗(from − toward +),τ⃗=p⃗×E⃗,∣τ∣=pEsin⁡θ\vec p = Q\vec\ell \quad \text{(from $-$ toward $+$)}, \qquad \vec\tau = \vec p \times \vec E, \quad |\tau| = pE\sin\thetap​=Qℓ(from − toward +),τ=p​×E,∣τ∣=pEsinθ

Energy (work to rotate against field):

U=−p⃗⋅E⃗=−pEcos⁡θU = -\vec p \cdot \vec E = -pE\cos\thetaU=−p​⋅E=−pEcosθ

Stable equilibrium: p⃗∥E⃗\vec p \parallel \vec Ep​∥E (UUU minimum).

Water, permanent vs induced dipoles · 물 분자·마이크로파

In H2O\mathrm{H_2O}H2​O, electrons spend more time near oxygen — oxygen side slightly negative, hydrogen side positive → permanent dipole moment.

Microwave ovens: oscillating electric field (≈ GHz) rotates/torques polar molecules; internal friction converts motion to heat. Non-polar molecules lack strong permanent ppp and heat less from this mechanism.

Induced dipoles: neutral molecules without permanent ppp can polarize in an external field (Ch.6 dielectrics connect here).

쌍극자: τ⃗=p⃗×E⃗\vec\tau=\vec p\times\vec Eτ=p​×E, U=−p⃗⋅E⃗U=-\vec p\cdot\vec EU=−p​⋅E. 물은 영구 쌍극자 → 마이크로파에서 장이 진동하며 분자를 돌리고 마찰로 가열. 유도 쌍극자는 외부 장에 순간 분극.

Test charge and superposition in practice · 시험 전하·실전 합

The test charge q′q'q′ must be small so it does not shift the sources. Once E⃗\vec EE is known, force on any charge is F⃗=q′E⃗\vec F = q'\vec EF=q′E — one field, many probes.

Component method: For each source, write dExdE_xdEx​, dEydE_ydEy​, dEzdE_zdEz​ (or use symmetry to skip integrals). Do not add magnitudes of EEE unless directions are identical.

Dipole in non-uniform fields · 비균일 장 (개념)

In a non-uniform field, a dipole can feel net force (not only torque) because ∣E∣|E|∣E∣ differs between +++ and −-− ends. Ch.2 exam focus is usually uniform EEE → F⃗tot=0\vec F_{\mathrm{tot}}=0Ftot​=0, τ⃗=p⃗×E⃗\vec\tau = \vec p\times\vec Eτ=p​×E.

Microwave detail: At ∼2.45 GHz\sim 2.45\,\mathrm{GHz}∼2.45GHz, the field oscillates; polar molecules (water) cannot fully align instantaneously but follow with lag — dielectric heating from repeated rotation and intermolecular friction. Oils with weak permanent dipoles heat less than water.

비균일 장에서는 쌍극자에 순힘도 가능(시험은 보통 균일 → 힘 0). 마이크로파: 진동 장 → 극성 분자(물) 반복 회전·마찰 → 발열. 유Permanent dipole 약한 기름은 덜 데워짐.
