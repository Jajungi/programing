Overview · 개요

Electrostatic potential VVV is potential energy per unit charge. The electric force is conservative, so work to move a charge depends only on endpoints. Potentials add as scalars — often easier than summing E⃗\vec EE vectors — and differentiation gives E⃗=−∇V\vec E = -\nabla VE=−∇V.

전위 V=U/qV=U/qV=U/q, 보존력 → 경로 무관. 스칼라 중첩 후 E⃗=−∇V\vec E=-\nabla VE=−∇V. 시험: 일·보존, 점전하 VVV, eV, 막대 축 ln⁡\lnln, 등전위, Faraday cage.

W=∫P1P2F⃗⋅dr⃗W = \int_{P_1}^{P_2} \vec F \cdot d\vec rW=∫P1​P2​​F⋅dr

For electric problems, F⃗=qE⃗\vec F = q\vec EF=qE, so work on charge qqq is tied to ∫E⃗⋅dl⃗\int \vec E\cdot d\vec l∫E⋅dl.

Conservative forces · 보존력

A force (depending only on position) is conservative if work between P1P_1P1​ and P2P_2P2​ is independent of path.

Equivalent forms:

  • Same endpoints ⇒ same WWW for any path.
  • Closed loop: ∮F⃗⋅dr⃗=0\displaystyle\oint \vec F\cdot d\vec r = 0∮F⋅dr=0.

Conservative: gravity, Coulomb/electric. Nonconservative: kinetic friction.

Shielding and Faraday cage · 차폐

Empty cavity in conductor at equilibrium: E=0E=0E=0 inside cavity. Proof sketch: suppose E⃗≠0\vec E \ne 0E=0 along a segment inside cavity. Complete a closed path: along field line in cavity, return through conductor (where E⃗=0\vec E=0E=0). Then ∮E⃗⋅dl⃗>0\oint \vec E\cdot d\vec l > 0∮E⋅dl>0, but electrostatic field is conservative — contradiction.

External uniform field on neutral conductor: induced surface charge makes E=0E=0E=0 in bulk and in cavity; exterior pattern distorts (lecture: sphere in uniform field). Faraday cage — safety from lightning, EMI demos.

Exam tip: Shielding is electrostatic equilibrium; time-varying fields need different analysis (later courses).

공동 E=0E=0E=0: 가정 장선 + 금속( E=0E=0E=0 ) → ∮E⃗⋅dl⃗≠0\oint\vec E\cdot d\vec l\ne 0∮E⋅dl=0 모순. Faraday cage. 정전기 평형 전제 — 시간 변하는 장은 별도.
