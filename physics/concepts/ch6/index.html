Overview · 개요

A capacitor stores charge and energy in the field between conductors. Capacitance CCC links charge magnitude to potential difference: Q=C ΔVQ = C\,\Delta VQ=CΔV. Topics: geometries (sphere, parallel plate, coaxial), series/parallel networks, energy, dielectrics (κ\kappaκ), and charge redistribution problems.
