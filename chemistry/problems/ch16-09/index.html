Ka of HBrO from salt pH

NaBrO pH에서 HBrO Ka

The pH of a 0.15 M aqueous solution of NaBrO is 10.7. What is the KaK_aKa​ for HBrO?
한글
NaBrO 0.15 M 수용액의 pH가 10.7이다. HBrO의 KaK_aKa​는?
Back to list
Hints
  1. BrO−\mathrm{BrO^-}BrO− is a weak base: find KbK_bKb​, then Ka=Kw/KbK_a=K_w/K_bKa​=Kw​/Kb​.
한글 힌트
  1. BrO−\mathrm{BrO^-}BrO−는 약염기: KbK_bKb​ 구한 뒤 Ka=Kw/KbK_a=K_w/K_bKa​=Kw​/Kb​.
Worked solution
Answer
Ka ≈ 6.0×10⁻⁹
한글
Ka ≈ 6.0×10⁻⁹
  1. Salt of weak acid. BrO−\mathrm{BrO^-}BrO− hydrolyzes: BrO−+H2O⇌HBrO+OH−\mathrm{BrO^- + H_2O \rightleftharpoons HBrO + OH^-}BrO−+H2​O⇌HBrO+OH−; find KbK_bKb​ from pH, then Ka=Kw/KbK_a = K_w/K_bKa​=Kw​/Kb​.
  2. From pH to pOH. pOH=14−10.7=3.3\mathrm{pOH} = 14 - 10.7 = 3.3pOH=14−10.7=3.3 → [OH−]=10−3.3=5.0×10−4 M[\mathrm{OH^-}] = 10^{-3.3} = 5.0\times 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{M}[OH−]=10−3.3=5.0×10−4M.
  3. 0 from ICE. Kb≈x2/C=(5.0×10−4)2/0.15=1.67×10−6K_b \approx x^2/C = (5.0\times 10^{-4})^2/0.15 = 1.67\times 10^{-6}Kb​≈x2/C=(5.0×10−4)2/0.15=1.67×10−6.
  4. Convert to 0. Ka=Kw/Kb=1.0×10−14/(1.67×10−6)≈6.0×10−9K_a = K_w/K_b = 1.0\times 10^{-14}/(1.67\times 10^{-6}) \approx 6.0\times 10^{-9}Ka​=Kw​/Kb​=1.0×10−14/(1.67×10−6)≈6.0×10−9 for HBrO.
