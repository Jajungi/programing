Ethanol solution — mole fraction, mass %, molality

에탄올 용액 — 몰분율·질량%·몰랄

A solution is made containing 50.0 g of ethanol (C2H5OH\mathrm{C_2H_5OH}C2​H5​OH) in 1000 g of H2O\mathrm{H_2O}H2​O. Calculate:

(a) the mole fraction of C2H5OH\mathrm{C_2H_5OH}C2​H5​OH
(b) the mass percent of C2H5OH\mathrm{C_2H_5OH}C2​H5​OH
(c) the molality of C2H5OH\mathrm{C_2H_5OH}C2​H5​OH
한글
에탄올(C2H5OH\mathrm{C_2H_5OH}C2​H5​OH) 50.0 g과 H2O\mathrm{H_2O}H2​O 1000 g으로 용액을 만들었다. 다음을 계산하라.

(a) C2H5OH\mathrm{C_2H_5OH}C2​H5​OH의 몰분율
(b) C2H5OH\mathrm{C_2H_5OH}C2​H5​OH의 질량 퍼센트
(c) C2H5OH\mathrm{C_2H_5OH}C2​H5​OH의 몰랄 농도

한글 힌트

Worked solution
Answer
(a) 0.0192 (b) 4.76% (c) 1.09 m
한글
(a) 0.0192 (b) 4.76% (c) 1.09 m
  1. Convert masses to moles. nEtOH=50.0/46.07=1.085 moln_{\mathrm{EtOH}} = 50.0/46.07 = 1.085\,\mathrm{mol}nEtOH​=50.0/46.07=1.085mol; nH2O=1000/18.02=55.49 moln_{\mathrm{H_2O}} = 1000/18.02 = 55.49\,\mathrm{mol}nH2​O​=1000/18.02=55.49mol.
  2. (a) Mole fraction. xEtOH=nEtOH/(nEtOH+nH2O)=1.085/(1.085+55.49)=0.0192x_{\mathrm{EtOH}} = n_{\mathrm{EtOH}}/(n_{\mathrm{EtOH}}+n_{\mathrm{H_2O}}) = 1.085/(1.085+55.49) = 0.0192xEtOH​=nEtOH​/(nEtOH​+nH2​O​)=1.085/(1.085+55.49)=0.0192. Ethanol is the minor component.
  3. (b) Mass percent. msolution=50.0+1000=1050 gm_{\mathrm{solution}} = 50.0 + 1000 = 1050\,\mathrm{g}msolution​=50.0+1000=1050g; mass %=(50.0/1050)×100=4.76%\mathrm{mass\,\%} = (50.0/1050)\times 100 = 4.76\%mass%=(50.0/1050)×100=4.76%.
  4. (c) Molality. Molality uses kg solvent only: m=nEtOH/1.000 kg=1.085/1.000=1.09 mm = n_{\mathrm{EtOH}}/1.000\,\mathrm{kg} = 1.085/1.000 = 1.09\,mm=nEtOH​/1.000kg=1.085/1.000=1.09m.
  5. Note. Mole fraction, mass %, and molality answer different “how much solute?” questions; do not mix solvent vs solution mass in the denominator.
