Henry’s law — CO₂ in a soft drink

헨리 법칙 — 탄산음료 CO₂

Calculate the concentration of CO2\mathrm{CO_2}CO2​ in a soft drink bottled with a partial pressure of CO2\mathrm{CO_2}CO2​ of 4.0 atm over the liquid at 25∘C25^\circ\mathrm{C}25∘C. The Henry’s law constant for CO2\mathrm{CO_2}CO2​ in water at this temperature is 3.4×10−2 mol L−1 atm−13.4\times 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}\,atm^{-1}}3.4×10−2molL−1atm−1.
한글
25∘C25^\circ\mathrm{C}25∘C에서 액체 위 CO2\mathrm{CO_2}CO2​ 분압이 4.0 atm인 탄산음료를 병입했다. 이 온도에서 물 속 CO2\mathrm{CO_2}CO2​의 헨리 상수는 3.4×10−2 mol L−1 atm−13.4\times 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}\,atm^{-1}}3.4×10−2molL−1atm−1이다. CO2\mathrm{CO_2}CO2​ 농도를 구하라.

Worked solution
Answer
0.136 M
한글
0.136 M
  1. Use Henry’s law. C=kHPC = k_H PC=kH​P gives molarity of dissolved gas from partial pressure PPP and constant kHk_HkH​.
  2. Insert values. kH=3.4×10−2 mol L−1 atm−1k_H = 3.4\times 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}\,atm^{-1}}kH​=3.4×10−2molL−1atm−1, PCO2=4.0 atmP_{\mathrm{CO_2}} = 4.0\,\mathrm{atm}PCO2​​=4.0atm.
  3. Multiply. C=(3.4×10−2)(4.0)=0.136 mol/LC = (3.4\times 10^{-2})(4.0) = 0.136\,\mathrm{mol/L}C=(3.4×10−2)(4.0)=0.136mol/L.
  4. Report. [CO2]=0.136 M[\mathrm{CO_2}] = 0.136\,\mathrm{M}[CO2​]=0.136M in the bottled soft drink at 25∘C25^\circ\mathrm{C}25∘C under 4.0 atm4.0\,\mathrm{atm}4.0atm headspace CO2\mathrm{CO_2}CO2​.
한글 해설
  1. 헨리 법칙 사용. C=kHPC = k_H PC=kH​P로 분압 PPP와 상수 kHk_HkH​에서 용존 기체 몰농도를 구한다.
  2. 값 대입. kH=3.4×10−2 mol L−1 atm−1k_H = 3.4\times 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}\,atm^{-1}}kH​=3.4×10−2molL−1atm−1, PCO2=4.0 atmP_{\mathrm{CO_2}} = 4.0\,\mathrm{atm}PCO2​​=4.0atm.
  3. 곱하기. C=(3.4×10−2)(4.0)=0.136 mol/LC = (3.4\times 10^{-2})(4.0) = 0.136\,\mathrm{mol/L}C=(3.4×10−2)(4.0)=0.136mol/L.
  4. 보고. 25∘C25^\circ\mathrm{C}25∘C, CO2\mathrm{CO_2}CO2​ 분압 4.0 atm4.0\,\mathrm{atm}4.0atm 조건에서 [CO2]=0.136 M[\mathrm{CO_2}] = 0.136\,\mathrm{M}[CO2​]=0.136M.
