Direction from Q vs K

$Q$ vs $K$로 진행 방향

For CO(g)+H2O(g)⇌CO2(g)+H2(g)\mathrm{CO(g)+H_2O(g)\rightleftharpoons CO_2(g)+H_2(g)}CO(g)+H2​O(g)⇌CO2​(g)+H2​(g), Kc=0.64K_c=0.64Kc​=0.64.
Experiment 1 initials: [CO]=[H2O]=0.0203 M[\mathrm{CO}]=[\mathrm{H_2O}]=0.0203\,\mathrm{M}[CO]=[H2​O]=0.0203M, [CO2]=[H2]=0.0040 M[\mathrm{CO_2}]=[\mathrm{H_2}]=0.0040\,\mathrm{M}[CO2​]=[H2​]=0.0040M.
In which direction does the reaction proceed to reach equilibrium?

Hints
  1. Compute QcQ_cQc​ and compare with KcK_cKc​.
한글 힌트
  1. QcQ_cQc​를 구해 KcK_cKc​와 비교.
Worked solution
Answer
Forward (toward products)
한글
정반응(생성물 쪽)
  1. Reaction quotient. Qc=[CO2][H2]/([CO][H2O])Q_c = [\mathrm{CO_2}][\mathrm{H_2}]/([\mathrm{CO}][\mathrm{H_2O}])Qc​=[CO2​][H2​]/([CO][H2​O]) — same form as KcK_cKc​ with initial concentrations.
  2. Compute 0. Qc=(0.0040)(0.0040)/[(0.0203)(0.0203)]=0.0388Q_c = (0.0040)(0.0040)/[(0.0203)(0.0203)] = 0.0388Qc​=(0.0040)(0.0040)/[(0.0203)(0.0203)]=0.0388.
  3. Compare to 0. Qc<KcQ_c < K_cQc​<Kc​ → too few products relative to equilibrium.
  4. Direction. The reaction proceeds forward (toward products) until QcQ_cQc​ approaches KcK_cKc​.
한글 해설
  1. 반응 지수. Qc=[CO2][H2]/([CO][H2O])Q_c = [\mathrm{CO_2}][\mathrm{H_2}]/([\mathrm{CO}][\mathrm{H_2O}])Qc​=[CO2​][H2​]/([CO][H2​O]) — KcK_cKc​와 같은 형태, 초기 농도 사용.
  2. 0 계산. Qc=(0.0040)(0.0040)/[(0.0203)(0.0203)]=0.0388Q_c = (0.0040)(0.0040)/[(0.0203)(0.0203)] = 0.0388Qc​=(0.0040)(0.0040)/[(0.0203)(0.0203)]=0.0388.
  3. 0와 비교. Qc<KcQ_c < K_cQc​<Kc​ → 평형 대비 생성물 부족.
  4. 진행 방향. 정반응(생성물 쪽)으로 진행해 QcQ_cQc​가 KcK_cKc​에 접근.
