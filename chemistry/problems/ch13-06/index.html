ppm of Zn²⁺

Zn²⁺ ppm 농도

A 2.5 g sample of groundwater was found to contain 5.4 µg of Zn2+\mathrm{Zn^{2+}}Zn2+. What is the concentration of Zn2+\mathrm{Zn^{2+}}Zn2+ in parts per million?
한글
지하수 시료 2.5 g에 Zn2+\mathrm{Zn^{2+}}Zn2+ 5.4 µg가 포함되어 있었다. Zn2+\mathrm{Zn^{2+}}Zn2+ 농도(ppm)는?
Back to list
Hints
  1. For dilute aqueous samples, ppm ≈μg\approx \mu\mathrm{g}≈μg solute per g sample.
한글 힌트
  1. 희석 수용액에서는 ppm ≈μg\approx \mu\mathrm{g}≈μg 용질 / g 시료.
Worked solution
Answer
2.16 ppm
한글
2.16 ppm
  1. Parts per million (ppm). For dilute aqueous samples, ppm≈(msolute (μg))/(msample (g))\mathrm{ppm} \approx (m_{\mathrm{solute\,(\mu g)}})/(m_{\mathrm{sample\,(g)}})ppm≈(msolute(μg)​)/(msample(g)​) — micrograms of analyte per gram of sample.
  2. Identify given values. Zn2+\mathrm{Zn^{2+}}Zn2+ mass =5.4 μg= 5.4\,\mu\mathrm{g}=5.4μg; sample mass =2.5 g= 2.5\,\mathrm{g}=2.5g.
  3. Compute ppm. ppm=5.4 μg/2.5 g=2.16 ppm\mathrm{ppm} = 5.4\,\mu\mathrm{g} / 2.5\,\mathrm{g} = 2.16\,\mathrm{ppm}ppm=5.4μg/2.5g=2.16ppm.
  4. Meaning. About 2.16 parts of Zn2+\mathrm{Zn^{2+}}Zn2+ per million parts of sample by mass — a trace concentration typical in groundwater analysis.
한글 해설
  1. ppm(질량) 정의. 희석 수용액 시료에서는 ppm≈msolute (μg)/msample (g)\mathrm{ppm} \approx m_{\mathrm{solute\,(\mu g)}} / m_{\mathrm{sample\,(g)}}ppm≈msolute(μg)​/msample(g)​ (시료 1 g당 용질 μg\mu\mathrm{g}μg).
  2. 주어진 값. Zn2+\mathrm{Zn^{2+}}Zn2+ =5.4 μg= 5.4\,\mu\mathrm{g}=5.4μg, 시료 =2.5 g= 2.5\,\mathrm{g}=2.5g.
  3. 계산. ppm=5.4 μg/2.5 g=2.16 ppm\mathrm{ppm} = 5.4\,\mu\mathrm{g} / 2.5\,\mathrm{g} = 2.16\,\mathrm{ppm}ppm=5.4μg/2.5g=2.16ppm.
  4. 의미. 시료 질량 100만 g당 약 2.16 g의 Zn2+\mathrm{Zn^{2+}}Zn2+ — 지하수에서 흔한 미량(trace) 농도이다.
