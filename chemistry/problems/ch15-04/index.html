Kc from equilibrium concentrations (HI)

HI 평형 농도에서 $K_c$

Gaseous HI partially decomposes: 2HI(g)⇌H2(g)+I2(g)2\mathrm{HI(g)\rightleftharpoons H_2(g)+I_2(g)}2HI(g)⇌H2​(g)+I2​(g). At equilibrium, [HI]=4.50×10−3 M[\mathrm{HI}]=4.50\times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{M}[HI]=4.50×10−3M and [H2]=[I2]=5.75×10−4 M[\mathrm{H_2}]=[\mathrm{I_2}]=5.75\times 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{M}[H2​]=[I2​]=5.75×10−4M. 한글
1.63×10⁻²
  1. Write 0 for the decomposition. 2HI⇌H2+I22\mathrm{HI \rightleftharpoons H_2 + I_2}2HI⇌H2​+I2​ → Kc=[H2][I2]/[HI]2K_c = [\mathrm{H_2}][\mathrm{I_2}]/[\mathrm{HI}]^2Kc​=[H2​][I2​]/[HI]2.
  2. Substitute equilibrium values. [HI]=4.50×10−3 M[\mathrm{HI}]=4.50\times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{M}[HI]=4.50×10−3M, [H2]=[I2]=5.75×10−4 M[\mathrm{H_2}]=[\mathrm{I_2}]=5.75\times 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{M}[H2​]=[I2​]=5.75×10−4M.
  3. Calculate. Kc=(5.75×10−4)2/(4.50×10−3)2=1.63×10−2K_c = (5.75\times 10^{-4})^2/(4.50\times 10^{-3})^2 = 1.63\times 10^{-2}Kc​=(5.75×10−4)2/(4.50×10−3)2=1.63×10−2.
  4. Interpret. Kc<1K_c < 1Kc​<1 favors reactants (HI) at this temperature — partial decomposition only.
