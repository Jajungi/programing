Freezing-point depression molar mass

어는점 내림 — 라우릴 알콜 몰질량

A solution of 5.00 g of lauryl alcohol in 0.100 kg of benzene freezes at 4.1∘C4.1^\circ\mathrm{C}4.1∘C. What is the molar mass of lauryl alcohol? (Benzene: normal freezing point 5.5∘C5.5^\circ\mathrm{C}5.5∘C, Kf=5.12 ∘C/mK_f=5.12\,^\circ\mathrm{C/m}Kf​=5.12∘C/m.)
한글
벤젠 0.100 kg에 라우릴 알콜 5.00 g을 녹인 용액의 어는점은 4.1∘C4.1^\circ\mathrm{C}4.1∘C이다. 라우릴 알콜의 몰질량은? (벤젠: 정상 어는점 5.5∘C5.5^\circ\mathrm{C}5.5∘C, Kf=5.12 ∘C/mK_f=5.12\,^\circ\mathrm{C/m}Kf​=5.12∘C/m.)

Hints
  1. ΔTf=Kfm\Delta T_f = K_f mΔTf​=Kf​m (nonelectrolyte, i=1i=1i=1)
  2. M=mass/nM=\mathrm{mass}/nM=mass/n
한글 힌트
  1. ΔTf=Kfm\Delta T_f = K_f mΔTf​=Kf​m (비전해질, i=1i=1i=1)
  2. M=질량/nM=\mathrm{질량}/nM=질량/n
Worked solution
Answer
183 g/mol
한글
183 g/mol
  1. Freezing-point depression. For a nonelectrolyte, ΔTf=Kfm\Delta T_f = K_f mΔTf​=Kf​m with i=1i=1i=1, where ΔTf=Tf∘−Tf\Delta T_f = T_f^\circ - T_fΔTf​=Tf∘​−Tf​ (pure solvent minus solution).
  2. Find 0. ΔTf=5.5−4.1=1.4 ∘C\Delta T_f = 5.5 - 4.1 = 1.4\,^\circ\mathrm{C}ΔTf​=5.5−4.1=1.4∘C.
  3. Solve for molality. m=ΔTf/Kf=1.4/5.12=0.273 mol/kgm = \Delta T_f/K_f = 1.4/5.12 = 0.273\,\mathrm{mol/kg}m=ΔTf​/Kf​=1.4/5.12=0.273mol/kg.
  4. Moles of solute. n=m×msolvent (kg)=0.273×0.100=0.0273 moln = m \times m_{\mathrm{solvent\,(kg)}} = 0.273 \times 0.100 = 0.0273\,\mathrm{mol}n=m×msolvent(kg)​=0.273×0.100=0.0273mol.
  5. Molar mass. M=msolute/n=5.00 g/0.0273 mol=183 g/molM = m_{\mathrm{solute}}/n = 5.00\,\mathrm{g}/0.0273\,\mathrm{mol} = 183\,\mathrm{g/mol}M=msolute​/n=5.00g/0.0273mol=183g/mol for lauryl alcohol.
