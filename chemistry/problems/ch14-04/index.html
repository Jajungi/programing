Rate constant from a second-order rate law

2차 속도식에서 속도 상수

The rate law is first order in C2H5Cl\mathrm{C_2H_5Cl}C2​H5​Cl and first order in OH−\mathrm{OH^-}OH−. When [C2H5Cl]=4.0×10−3 M[\mathrm{C_2H_5Cl}]=4.0\times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{M}[C2​H5​Cl]=4.0×10−3M and [OH−]=2.5×10−2 M[\mathrm{OH^-}]=2.5\times 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{M}[OH−]=2.5×10−2M, the rate at 310 K is 5.20×10−2 M/s5.20\times 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{M/s}5.20×10−2M/s.

(a) What is the value of the rate constant?
(b) What are the units of kkk?
(c) What happens to the rate if [OH−][\mathrm{OH^-}][OH−] is cut in half?
(d) What happens if both reactant concentrations are cut in half?
한글
속도식은 C2H5Cl\mathrm{C_2H_5Cl}C2​H5​Cl에 1차, OH−\mathrm{OH^-}OH−에 1차이다. [C2H5Cl]=4.0×10−3 M[\mathrm{C_2H_5Cl}]=4.0\times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{M}[C2​H5​Cl]=4.0×10−3M, [OH−]=2.5×10−2 M[\mathrm{OH^-}]=2.5\times 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{M}[OH−]=2.5×10−2M일 때 310 K에서 속도는 5.20×10−2 M/s5.20\times 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{M/s}5.20×10−2M/s이다.

(a) 속도 상수 kkk는?
(b) kkk의 단위는?
(c) [OH−][\mathrm{OH^-}][OH−]만 절반이면 속도는?
(d) 두 농도 모두 절반이면?

Answer
k = 520 M⁻¹ s⁻¹
한글
k = 520 M⁻¹ s⁻¹
  1. Rate law. rate=k[C2H5Cl][OH−]\mathrm{rate} = k[\mathrm{C_2H_5Cl}][\mathrm{OH^-}]rate=k[C2​H5​Cl][OH−] — overall second order (first order in each reactant).
  2. (a) Solve for 0. k=rate/([C2H5Cl][OH−])=(5.20×10−2)/[(4.0×10−3)(2.5×10−2)]k = \mathrm{rate}/([\mathrm{C_2H_5Cl}][\mathrm{OH^-}]) = (5.20\times 10^{-2})/[(4.0\times 10^{-3})(2.5\times 10^{-2})]k=rate/([C2​H5​Cl][OH−])=(5.20×10−2)/[(4.0×10−3)(2.5×10−2)].
  3. Numeric 0. k=5.2×102 M−1s−1k = 5.2\times 10^{2}\,\mathrm{M^{-1}s^{-1}}k=5.2×102M−1s−1.
  4. (b) Units. Second-order overall → M−1 s−1\mathrm{M^{-1}\,s^{-1}}M−1s−1 (or L mol−1 s−1\mathrm{L\,mol^{-1}\,s^{-1}}Lmol−1s−1).
  5. (c) Halve 0: Rate is proportional to [OH−][\mathrm{OH^-}][OH−] → rate halved.
  6. (d) Halve both concentrations: Rate ∝[C2H5Cl][OH−]\propto [\mathrm{C_2H_5Cl}][\mathrm{OH^-}]∝[C2​H5​Cl][OH−] → each factor 1/21/21/2 → rate one-fourth.
