Doubling solvent and solute mass

용매·용질 질량 2배와 몰랄 농도

Suppose you take a solution and add more solvent so that the original mass of solvent is doubled. You then add more solute so that the original mass of solute is doubled. What happens to the molality of the final solution compared to the original molality?

(a) It is doubled.
(b) It is decreased by half.
(c) It is unchanged.
(d) It will increase or decrease depending on the molar mass of the solute.
(e) There is no way to tell without knowing the molar mass of the solute.
한글
어떤 용액에 용매를 더 넣어 원래 용매 질량이 두 배가 되게 한 뒤, 용질도 더 넣어 원래 용질 질량이 두 배가 되게 했다. 최종 용액의 몰랄 농도는 처음 몰랄 농도와 비교해 어떻게 되는가?

(a) 두 배가 된다.
(b) 절반이 된다.
(c) 변하지 않는다.
(d) 용질의 몰질량에 따라 증가하거나 감소한다.
(e) 용질 몰질량을 알아야 판단할 수 없다.

  2. Both numerator and denominator scale by 2.
한글 힌트
  1. m=nsolute/kgsolventm = n_{\mathrm{solute}} / \mathrm{kg_{solvent}}m=nsolute​/kgsolvent​
  2. 분자·분모가 모두 2배로 스케일된다.
Worked solution
Answer
(c)
한글
(c)
  1. Write molality. m=nsolute/msolvent (kg)m = n_{\mathrm{solute}}/m_{\mathrm{solvent\,(kg)}}m=nsolute​/msolvent(kg)​. Both moles of solute and kilograms of solvent appear in this ratio.
  2. Track the doubling. Original: m0=n/mm_0 = n/mm0​=n/m. After doubling solute mass, moles become 2n2n2n (same molar mass). After doubling solvent mass, msolvent=2mm_{\mathrm{solvent}} = 2mmsolvent​=2m.
  3. Form the new molality. mnew=2n/(2m)=n/m=m0m_{\mathrm{new}} = 2n/(2m) = n/m = m_0mnew​=2n/(2m)=n/m=m0​. The factor of 2 cancels in numerator and denominator.
  4. Why molar mass does not matter here. We only scaled masses; the ratio of moles to kg solvent is unchanged regardless of solute identity.
  5. Conclusion. Molality is unchanged → choice (c).
한글 해설
  1. 몰랄 농도 식. m=nsolute/msolvent (kg)m = n_{\mathrm{solute}}/m_{\mathrm{solvent\,(kg)}}m=nsolute​/msolvent(kg)​. 분자(용질 mol)와 분모(용매 kg) 모두 이 비에 들어간다.
  2. 2배 변화 추적. 처음: m0=n/mm_0 = n/mm0​=n/m. 용질 질량 2배 → 같은 몰질량이면 mol은 2n2n2n. 용매 질량 2배 → msolvent=2mm_{\mathrm{solvent}} = 2mmsolvent​=2m.
  3. 새 몰랄 농도. mnew=2n/(2m)=n/m=m0m_{\mathrm{new}} = 2n/(2m) = n/m = m_0mnew​=2n/(2m)=n/m=m0​. 분자·분모의 2가 약분된다.
  4. 몰질량이 왜 상관없는가. 여기서는 질량만 같은 배율로 키웠을 뿐이므로, mol/kg 비율은 용질 종류와 무관하게 불변이다.
  5. 결론. 몰랄 농도는 변하지 않음 → 선택 (c).
