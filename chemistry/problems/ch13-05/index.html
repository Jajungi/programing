Mass percentage of glucose

포도당 질량 퍼센트

A solution is made by dissolving 13.5 g of glucose (C6H12O6\mathrm{C_6H_{12}O_6}C6​H12​O6​) in 0.100 kg of water. What is the mass percentage of solute in this solution?
한글
13.5 g의 포도당(C6H12O6\mathrm{C_6H_{12}O_6}C6​H12​O6​)을 0.100 kg의 물에 녹여 용액을 만들었다. 용질의 질량 퍼센트는?
Back to list
Hints
  1. mass % =(mass solute/mass solution)×100= (\mathrm{mass\,solute}/\mathrm{mass\,solution})\times 100=(masssolute/masssolution)×100
  2. 0.100 kg water = 100 g
한글 힌트
  1. 질량 % =(용질 질량/용액 질량)×100= (\mathrm{용질\,질량}/\mathrm{용액\,질량})\times 100=(용질질량/용액질량)×100
  2. 0.100 kg 물 = 100 g
Worked solution
Answer
11.9%
한글
11.9%
  1. Mass percent definition. mass %=(msolute/msolution)×100\mathrm{mass\,\%} = (m_{\mathrm{solute}}/m_{\mathrm{solution}}) \times 100mass%=(msolute​/msolution​)×100. You need total solution mass, not just solvent mass.
  2. Convert units. 0.100 kg0.100\,\mathrm{kg}0.100kg water =100 g= 100\,\mathrm{g}=100g water. Solute mass is 13.5 g13.5\,\mathrm{g}13.5g glucose.
  3. Total solution mass. msolution=13.5+100=113.5 gm_{\mathrm{solution}} = 13.5 + 100 = 113.5\,\mathrm{g}msolution​=13.5+100=113.5g.
  4. Substitute. mass %=(13.5/113.5)×100=11.9%\mathrm{mass\,\%} = (13.5/113.5) \times 100 = 11.9\%mass%=(13.5/113.5)×100=11.9%.
  5. Check. Solute is a modest fraction of the total mass; a result near 12%12\%12% is reasonable for this recipe.
